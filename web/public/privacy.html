Privacy

Last updated 3 September 2026

Flagrante is a tool for people who are about to be held to a European regulation. It would be absurd to run it on a site that quietly did the opposite of what it preaches, so this page is specific rather than generic, and short enough to actually read.

Your SBOM is never stored. When you scan one here it is parsed in memory, checked, and dropped when the request ends. It is not written to disk, not logged, not retained, and not sent anywhere else. There is no storage layer in the scanner at all, so there is nothing there to leak, subpoena, or breach. If you would rather it never left your machine, pip install flagrante does exactly the same thing locally.

Who is responsible

Flagrante is operated by an individual, not yet a company. For anything on this page — access, deletion, objection, or a question — write to privacy@flagrante.dev. That address is read by a person, and it is the same person who would fix whatever you report.

What is deliberately not collected

  • Your SBOM, or any part of it. Not the file, not the component list, not the package names. The survey record keeps only counts.
  • Your IP address. It is used in transit to apply rate limits and is never written down.
  • Cookies. There are none. The random number lives in your browser's own storage and never travels in a request header.
  • Anything from advertising or tracking networks. There are no third-party scripts on this site, no analytics product, no pixels, no ad tech. The two fonts are the only external request the page makes.

Where it lives

In a Cloudflare D1 database with its primary location in Western Europe. That was a deliberate choice: most of the people this is built for are EU manufacturers, and it would be strange to sell European compliance while keeping European data somewhere else. Cloudflare processes it on our behalf and does nothing else with it. Nobody else receives it, and it is never sold, shared, or used to build a profile.

Your rights, and how to actually use them

If you are in the EU or UK you can ask for a copy of what we hold, ask for it to be corrected, ask for it to be deleted, withdraw consent, or object. In practice there are two ways, and the first one is instant:

Delete this browser's record now

Checking what is stored for this browser...

This erases the random number from your browser and deletes the matching row from the database. No email, no ticket, no waiting. It cannot delete a waitlist entry, because that is tied to your email address rather than to this browser.

For anything else — a copy of your data, deleting a waitlist entry, or a complaint — write to privacy@flagrante.dev. We will answer within 30 days, which is what the GDPR requires, and normally much sooner because there is very little data to look through.

You also have the right to complain to your national data protection authority if you think we have got this wrong.

Changes

If this page changes materially, the date at the top changes with it, and anyone on the waitlist gets told. This site is open source, so the full history of this page is public and you can check what changed rather than take our word for it.
